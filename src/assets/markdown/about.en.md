The Federal Personnel Tracking Tool provides detailed data visualizations on headcounts and full-time equivalents (FTEs) in the federal public service. It enables parliamentarians and the public to explore recent federal workforce developments and historical staffing trends across government departments and agencies. Users can compare headcount and FTE trends over time for the overall government or by specific departments, as well as by job tenure.

Personnel costs are the largest component of federal operating spending, making the size of the federal public service a key driver of overall government expenses. This tool provides timely, accessible data to support parliamentarians’ scrutiny of the Government’s staffing and spending decisions.
