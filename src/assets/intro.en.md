### Overview

The Federal Personnel Tracking Tool provides detailed data visualizations on headcounts and full-time equivalents (FTEs) in the federal public service. This interactive tool allows parliamentarians and the public to gain a deeper understanding of the recent dynamics in the federal government workforce as well as the varying historical trends in staffing across different departments and agencies. Users can compare trends in headcounts and FTEs across time for the overall government or by specific departments, as well as by job tenure.

Personnel costs are the largest component of federal operating spending, therefore the size of the federal public service is a key driver of overall government spending. The purpose of this tool is to provide timely data that support parliamentarians’ scrutiny of the Government’s staffing and spending decisions.

### Notes

- The information provided is for the Federal Public Service (i.e., Core Public Administration and Separate Agencies). The Core Public Administration consists of the departments and agencies named in schedules I and IV of the Financial Administration Act (FAA). The Treasury Board is the employer of this segment of the Federal Public Service.
- Employees on leave without pay as well as employees locally engaged outside of Canada are not included.
